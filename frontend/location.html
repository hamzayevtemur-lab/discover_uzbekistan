Uzbekistan Transport Guide & Apps
Essential transport tips, ride-hailing apps & safety for travelers
🚕 Yandex Go (Dominant App) Works within Samarkand, Tashkent, Bukhara, Namangan, Fergana, Andijan. Note: Intra-city only — cannot book intercity rides in-app.

❌ Uber Notice Uber does NOT operate in Uzbekistan (left in 2018). Please download Yandex Go or MyTaxi instead.
Tourist-Trap Warning: Walk past aggressive unofficial drivers at train stations & airports quoting inflated prices. Use Yandex Go, MyTaxi, or an official taxi stand.
🚕 Street Taxis: Look for a "TAXI" sign in the windshield. Taxis are cash-only; agree on the fare before getting in. Hotels & restaurants will call a cab for you on request.
Local Dispatch: Ask your hotel or guesthouse staff for current local taxi dispatch numbers — these vary by neighborhood.
🚄 Train Travel (Afrosiyob): High-speed train is the best way between Tashkent, Samarkand & Bukhara. Book early via Railway.uz as tickets sell out fast!
🚌 Local Bus & Shared Taxis: Local city buses have unpredictable schedules. For intercity gaps without trains, shared taxis (e.g. Samarkand ↔ Shakhrisabz) depart near Ulugbek stand (Tashkent-bound) or Navoi stand (Bukhara-bound).
